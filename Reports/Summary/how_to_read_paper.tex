\section{Introduction}
Paper reading is a must for researcher or master students.They are seldom taught the skill to learn it. This paper guide the reader on the approach to read any research paper in thorough and non daunting way.
\section{Content}
The paper discuss a three pass approach to read any paper. And the method to choose and access the related papers.

 \subsection{First pass:-(5-10 minutes)}This pass is all about a quick scan to get a bird's eye-view on the paper. Here you will read the title, abstract, introduction, section and subsection headings and conclusion.Finally just a quick look through reference to see with what you are familiar with.
From it you will get 5 C's(Category, Clarity,Context, Contributions and correctness )

 \subsection{Second pass:-(1-2 hours)}
In this pass you will dive deeper into the paper to read with greater care . You will ignore proofs here. Here you will go through figures,diagram and other illustrations. Mark unread relevant reference to go later. This level of detail is enough for grasping the content. It is still not enough for research. 

 \subsection{Third pass:-(5-6 to more hours)}
This is the pass where you will be in the shoe of the author and try to reimplement the paper virtually. Here you will make the same assumption as the author , recreate the work which will not only give you the paper's innovation but also the between the line shortcoming and assumptions. Try to challenge every assumptions. If you like an idea add it to your tool box. 
\subsection{Finding Papers:-}
For applying the above method you need to find the research paper first.You can use three pass in finding the research paper. 
\subsubsection{First pass:- }
In a academic search engines using keyword find three to five recent paper and on it do a first pass to get an idea and find the related works sections.
\subsubsection{Second pass:- }
Find related citations or author's name in the bibliography. Download the papers and go to author's website to see there publication and the conference they are publishing.
\subsubsection{Third pass:-}
From the conferences website find related paper and give it first pass to know the related content. Give at least two passes to get familiarize in all concepts.
\section{Conclusion:-}
The author has used it for 15 years to get a depth of evaluation of paper based on his need and time provided. So for students and reviewer to get optimum out of their time are highly advised to follow this method.
